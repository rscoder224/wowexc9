Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding wholesale orders and delivery

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for wholesale delivery? +

Minimum order quantities depend on the product category. Standard FMCG and beverage bulk orders usually start at case/box levels. Contact our sales team for category-specific MOQs.

Which delivery regions does Sln Distributor cover? +

We primarily service Hosakote, Bengaluru (Rural), and neighboring commercial districts across Karnataka through our local transport network.

What payment options do you accept for commercial orders? +

We support electronic bank transfers (NEFT/RTGS), UPI payments, bank cheques, and cash-on-delivery for authorized recurring accounts.

Are all products genuine manufacturer stock? +

Yes, Sln Distributor deals exclusively in authentic, original products procured directly from recognized FMCG brand channels.
