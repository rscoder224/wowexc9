Workflow Transparency

Our Manufacturing Process

From raw fiber selection to final delivery, step inside our meticulous creation lifecycle.

Step 01

Material Selection

We source raw cotton, wool, and silk from certified eco-friendly farms, validating staple length and purity.

Step 02

Fabric Processing & Spinning

Fibers are spun into high-tensile threads using low-energy automated machinery for uniform consistency.

Step 03

Design & Weaving

Master artisans collaborate with digital CAD systems to produce rich textures, jacquards, and prints.

Step 04

Quality Inspection

Every meter undergoes stringent tensile, color-fastness, and flaw checks under industrial lighting.

Step 05

Packaging & Dispatch

Fabrics are vacuum sealed and securely boxed for expedited dispatch to global destinations.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information voluntarily provided by users when filling out inquiries, requesting swatch samples, or contacting our client care team. This includes your Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Corporate Name, and Product Specifications.

2. How We Use Your Data

The collected data is exclusively utilized to respond to wholesale inquiries, fulfill order shipments, send order updates, improve custom fabric formulations, and optimize digital user experience across our platforms.

3. Data Security

Wowexch9 implements advanced SSL encryption, firewall standards, and restricted access protocols to guarantee that client personal information remains secure from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.
